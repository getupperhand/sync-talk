What sync is

Every song is two rights.Sync needs both.

Right one

The master

The recording itself. Owned by a label, or by you.

+
Right two

The composition

The song underneath it. Owned by the writers and their publishers.

A sync license is permission to put both against picture. A film, a show, an ad, a trailer, a game.

one "no" from either side and it's dead
Where it was · 2008

The doorhad aguest list.

Music supervisors called major labels, big publishers and a handful of agents. If you were unsigned, nobody was asking.

And clearing one song could take weeks. Every writer, every owner, every signature.

What we built · Music Dealers

So we putthe brief whereany artistcould see it.

01 · Open briefs

A brand posts the mood, the references and the budget. Any artist can answer.

02 · Pre-cleared, one-stop

Artists confirmed they controlled the master and the publishing. A brand could say yes the same day.

03 · Non-exclusive

Artists kept their copyrights and could license the same song anywhere else.

How you get paid

You getpaid twice.

the third one changes careers
  • 01The sync fee. Up front, negotiated, for the right to use it. Split between the master side and the publishing side.
  • 02The royalties. Every time it airs, your PRO collects performance royalties for the writers.
  • 03The aftershock. Streams, Shazams, followers, and the next supervisor who heard it.

Where it's going

AI can makea track. Itcan't own one.

The US Copyright Office says fully AI-generated work can't be copyrighted. A brand putting millions behind a campaign wants a song somebody owns, can license, and will stand behind.

Search gets faster. Budgets get smaller. There are more places to be heard. The premium goes to the one thing a model doesn't have: a person with a story.

be findable. be clearable. be human.
How to get placed · part one

The song thatclears fastestwins.

  • Know who owns what. Splits in writing, the day you write it.
  • Be one-stop if you can. One email, one yes.
  • Bounce the instrumental and the stems. Every time.
  • Metadata in the file: writers, PRO, contact.
  • Register the song with your PRO.
  • No uncleared samples. Not even a little one.
How to get placed · part two

Three doors in.

  • 01Libraries. Non-exclusive is a fine place to start. It's a volume game, so keep feeding it.
  • 02Sync agents. They pitch for a cut of the fee. They want a catalog, not a single.
  • 03Music supervisors. Read the credits. Be short, be relevant, send a link and never an attachment.
Pitch the scene, not the song.
